Data-governance note
Fig. 2 shows eight de-identified same-patient CT-radiograph pairs from the
BIMCV-COVID19 releases. The BIMCV-COVID19 Research Use Agreement (clause 5)
requires specific prior written permission to publish or reproduce any portion
of the dataset; a draft permission request is in docs/bimcv_permission_request.txt.
No individual image files or dataset copies are included beyond the figure.
Per-image model predictions are not included because they are indexed by BIMCV
and MIDRC identifiers; the manuscript states that they are available on
request.

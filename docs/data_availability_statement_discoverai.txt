Data Availability Statement (Discover Artificial Intelligence submission)

Selection: Yes. I used or generated research data in this study.

Statement text:
The BIMCV-COVID19+ imaging data used in this study are available from the
original data custodians (Valencian Region Medical ImageBank) at
https://bimcv.cipf.es/bimcv-projects/bimcv-covid19 under the BIMCV-COVID19
Research Use Agreement:
https://bimcv.cipf.es/bimcv-projects/bimcv-covid19/bimcv-covid19-dataset-research-use-agreement-2/.
The data are de-identified, and the agreement prohibits data sharing,
re-identification, and publication or reproduction of any portion of the
dataset without specific prior written permission. We therefore do not
redistribute image pixels or individual image files. The manuscript reports
only derived aggregate results and the four-row anonymized case-level gate
table (teacher confidence, prediction, ground truth, and gate outcome) with no
patient identifiers. The patient-level split definitions, aggregate result
tables, and analysis manifests that support the findings of this study are
available at
https://github.com/mabo1215/JDCNET.git.

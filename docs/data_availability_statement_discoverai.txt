Data Availability Statement (Discover Artificial Intelligence submission)

Selection: Yes. I used or generated research data in this study.

Statement text:
The BIMCV-COVID19 imaging data (BIMCV-COVID19+ and BIMCV-COVID19- releases)
used in this study are available from the data custodians (Valencian Region
Medical ImageBank) at https://bimcv.cipf.es/bimcv-projects/bimcv-covid19 under
the BIMCV-COVID19 Research Use Agreement:
https://bimcv.cipf.es/bimcv-projects/bimcv-covid19/bimcv-covid19-dataset-research-use-agreement-2/.
The data are de-identified, and the agreement prohibits data sharing,
re-identification, and publication or reproduction of any portion of the
dataset without specific prior written permission. We therefore do not
redistribute the dataset or individual image files; Fig. 2 of the manuscript
shows eight de-identified same-patient CT-radiograph example pairs for
illustration only. The historical paired subset is part of the
public COVID-19 image data collection (Cohen et al.), and the external
radiographs are available from MIDRC (https://www.midrc.org) under its data
use terms. The patient-level split definitions, archived aggregate result
records, analysis plan, and analysis scripts that support the findings of
this study are available at https://github.com/mabo1215/JDCNET. Per-image
model predictions, which are needed for the patient-level, external, and
teacher analyses and are indexed by BIMCV and MIDRC identifiers, are
available from the corresponding author on reasonable request, subject to the
source data-use terms.

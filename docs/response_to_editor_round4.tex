\section*{Editor (Quality Check)}

\begin{rcomment}
1. Data availability statement (in system): When submitting your revised
manuscript, please select `yes' under the data availability declaration. This
ensures that your data statement will be automatically extracted from your
manuscript. Please make sure that Data availability statement in system and
manuscript exactly matches.
\end{rcomment}

\textbf{Response.} We have selected ``Yes. I used or generated research data
in this study'' under the data availability declaration in the submission
system. The statement entered in the system and the ``Data availability''
paragraph in the Declarations of the revised manuscript are now identical,
word for word.

The statement previously entered in the system had not been updated after the
last revision. It did not mention the historical paired subset, the external
MIDRC radiographs, or the availability of per-image predictions on request,
and it did not mention the example images in Fig.~2. We replaced it with the
manuscript statement. To make an exact match possible, the manuscript
paragraph no longer contains numbered citations; the two data descriptors are
given by their DOIs instead and remain cited in the body of the paper.
Following the journal's guidance on data availability statements, each
dataset is identified by name, repository, and a persistent identifier where
one exists. The statement now reads:

\begin{quote}
The BIMCV-COVID19 imaging data (BIMCV-COVID19+ and BIMCV-COVID19- releases;
data descriptor: \url{https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2006.01174}) used in this
study are available from the data custodians (Valencian Region
Medical ImageBank) at \url{https://bimcv.cipf.es/bimcv-projects/bimcv-covid19}
under the BIMCV-COVID19 Research Use Agreement:
\url{https://bimcv.cipf.es/bimcv-projects/bimcv-covid19/bimcv-covid19-dataset-research-use-agreement-2/}.
The data are de-identified, and the agreement prohibits data sharing,
re-identification, and publication or reproduction of any portion of the
dataset without specific prior written permission. We therefore do not
redistribute the dataset or individual image files; Fig.~2 shows eight
de-identified same-patient CT-radiograph example pairs for illustration only.
The historical paired subset is part of the public COVID-19 image data
collection (Cohen et al., \url{https://doi.org/10.59275/j.melba.2020-48g7}),
which is openly available at
\url{https://github.com/ieee8023/covid-chestxray-dataset}. The external
radiographs are available from the Medical Imaging and Data Resource Center
(MIDRC; \url{https://www.midrc.org}) under its data use terms. The
patient-level split definitions, archived aggregate result records, analysis
plan, and analysis scripts that support the findings of this study are
available at \url{https://github.com/mabo1215/JDCNET}. Per-image model
predictions, which are needed for the patient-level, external, and teacher
analyses and are indexed by BIMCV and MIDRC identifiers, are available from
the corresponding author on reasonable request, subject to the source
data-use terms.
\end{quote}

The other declarations entered in the system (funding, competing interests,
and acknowledgements) already match the manuscript and are unchanged.

\textbf{Other changes to the manuscript.} (i) In Section~4.11, the sentence
on data handling now uses the same wording as the statement (``do not
redistribute the dataset or individual image files'', with Fig.~2 described as
illustrative examples), replacing ``do not redistribute pixel data''. (ii) In
Fig.~2, text annotations burned into the radiographs (orientation markers and
acquisition labels) are now masked with solid patches, and the caption states
this. No other content, result, or conclusion has changed.

Subject: Request for written permission to reproduce a de-identified image montage from BIMCV-COVID19 in a journal article

Dear BIMCV team,

We are preparing a revised research article for Discover Artificial Intelligence
(Springer Nature), titled "JDCNet Uses Confidence Gated Privileged Modality
Distillation for Cost Preserving X Ray Inference" (Submission ID
bf05c1ad-f7f2-44f8-a235-de9eeb659798). The study is a secondary analysis of the
BIMCV-COVID19+ and BIMCV-COVID19- releases, used under the BIMCV-COVID19 Research
Use Agreement, and cites de la Iglesia Vaya et al. (arXiv:2006.01174) as required
by Clause 2.

Clause 5 of the agreement requires specific prior written permission to publish
or reproduce any portion of the dataset. We would like to request this
permission for one figure:

  - Content: eight same-patient pairs, each showing one axial CT mid-slice and
    the matched frontal chest radiograph, arranged as a single montage.
  - Purpose: to show readers the two imaging modalities and the pairing used in
    the study. The figure is not used for diagnosis and is not a dataset.
  - De-identification: no patient identifiers, session identifiers, file names,
    DICOM metadata, dates, or burned-in text are shown. Images can be shown at
    reduced resolution if you prefer.
  - Distribution: only within the published article (and its preprint, if any).
    No individual image files are redistributed.

If permission cannot be granted, we will remove the figure. We would also be
grateful to know whether you would like a specific acknowledgement wording.

Thank you for making BIMCV-COVID19 available to the research community.
